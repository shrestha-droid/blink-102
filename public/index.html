    What am I looking at?

    1. The picture is the same small patch of sky, photographed by NASA's SPHEREx telescope on different dates. It flips between dates, so anything that moved or changed brightness catches your eye.
    2. The row of small pictures is one picture per date. Each is built by stacking every exposure SPHEREx took of this spot during that pass.
    3. The colour bar is infrared wavelength, 0.75 to 5.0 µm in 102 steps. Drag the window to choose which colours go into the picture. Some objects only exist at one end.
    4. Click a star to measure it in every exposure: its spectrum, how its brightness changed, and how fast it moves.
    5. Green rings are stars already in catalogues, drawn where they should be on each date. A dot that moves with no ring is worth a second look.
